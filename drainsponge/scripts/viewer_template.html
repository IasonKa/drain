What is computed here, and what the package contains

This viewer shows the reduced-order model (axisymmetric, linear poroelastic sponge + lubrication film + slots as ducts + hole with viscous and orifice losses + contact), solved in Python and revolved onto the 3D window. It is a preview, not the OpenFOAM/FEBio result. The ring slots are modelled as flow paths; tissue sagging into the slots is only captured by the 3D model.

Scenarios: free liquid around = the gap is open to a pool of wound liquid at body pressure; no free liquid = the only liquid is inside the tissue (the case that decides whether tissue is sucked into the hole).

3D models: out/cases/<design>_<scenario>/ holds the OpenFOAM cases (Darcy–Brinkman, ready to run) and a FEBio biphasic model; hpc/ runs the full two-way OpenFOAM ⇄ FEBio coupling on ETH Euler, window by window from the last saved state.

Placeholder inputs: tissue E = kPa, k = m⁴/(N·s), suction kPa, residual film under contact 3 µm. Replace with clinical values.
